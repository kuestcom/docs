## Choose your installation path

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Guided launch" icon="rocket" href="/launch/overview" cta="Start the guided path">
    Connect GitHub, Vercel, Reown, and Supabase. Kuest prepares the repository and deployment for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual installation" icon="server" href="/manual-installation/overview" cta="Compare deployment targets">
    Deploy the frontend on infrastructure you manage and choose your own database, storage, scheduler, and release process.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## What you will manage

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Deployment" icon="cloud-cog">
    Repository, hosting, database, asset storage, secrets, domain, monitoring, and scheduled jobs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Site experience" icon="palette">
    Brand, theme, languages, categories, featured markets, integrations, fees, and legal controls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operations" icon="activity">
    Admin wallets, event review, market creation, resolution, user support, and production alerts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kuest connection" icon="network">
    Credentials and service URLs that connect your site to shared trading, market data, wallet, and resolution infrastructure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Recommended reading order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the architecture">
    Read [How Kuest Works](/concepts/architecture) before choosing infrastructure or exposing the site to users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the site">
    Use [Guided Launch](/launch/overview) or choose a target in [Manual Installation](/manual-installation/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the admin panel">
    Start with [General](/admin/general), then review [Integrations](/admin/integrations), [Theme](/admin/theme), and [Affiliate & Fees](/admin/affiliate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare production operations">
    Verify admin access, scheduled jobs, monitoring, market creation, and resolution before inviting traders.
  </Step>
</Steps>
