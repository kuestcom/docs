<Info>
This page controls how your prediction market splits trading fees between your platform and affiliates.
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Maker and taker fees (%)" icon="percent" defaultOpen>
    Configure the operator component for maker and taker fills separately. Each field accepts decimals such as `2.00`, with a range from `0%` to `9%`. The helper text combines it with the live Kuest execution fee so you can review the total rate shown to traders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Affiliate share (%)" icon="hand-coins">
    This is not a percent of trade volume. It is the percent of your configured trading fee that gets shared with affiliates. Example input: `50` means affiliates receive half of your configured trading fee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fee wallet receiver" icon="wallet">
    The Polygon fee wallet must match your deployed admin deposit wallet. The field is read-only; use <strong>Use my deposit wallet</strong> when the saved receiver has not been set yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How the calculation works" icon="calculator">
    The trader's total fee combines the live Kuest execution fee with your configured operator component. Affiliate sharing applies only to the operator component: `affiliate payout = operator fee × affiliate share`. Your site keeps the remainder of that component.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claimable fees" icon="coins">
    The page shows the configured receiver's claimable balance across supported exchanges. Connect the receiver wallet and withdraw once at least `$1` is claimable. Deposit-wallet claims may ask for a trading signature first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Example" icon="coins">
    For `$100` of filled taker notional with a `2.00%` operator component and a `50%` affiliate share, the operator component is `$2.00`. The affiliate target is `$1.00`, and your site retains `$1.00`. The live Kuest execution fee is calculated separately and added to the trader's total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Overview cards and performance table" icon="table-properties">
    The page also shows who is actually sending traffic and earning. Expect totals for referrals, trading volume, and affiliate fees, plus a performance table with avatar, affiliate code, referrals, volume, and fees per partner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
Affiliate commission is taken from the trading fee at execution, not directly from traded volume.
</Warning>
