Kuest charges takers with a category-specific curve. Your site receives a configurable share of the final taker fee and can share that operator amount with affiliates. Maker fees are a separate optional flat fee.

<Info>
Kuest maker fee and the recommended operator maker fee are both `0%`. The operator taker setting is a share of the final fee, not a flat percentage of trade value.
</Info>

## Kuest fee base

The Kuest component is calculated for every fill:

```text
Kuest fee base = shares × rate × [price × (1 - price)]^exponent
```

`price` is expressed from `0` to `1`, and `exponent` is currently `1`. The curve is highest at `50¢` and symmetric around the midpoint.

| Market category | Rate | Maker rebate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Crypto | `0.0441` | `20%` |
| Sports | `0.0315` | `15%` |
| Finance, Politics, Mentions, Tech, Geopolitics | `0.0252` | `25%` |
| Economics, Culture, Weather, General | `0.0315` | `25%` |

This is Kuest's own versioned table. Native and mirrored markets both use the local category. An unclassified market remains fee-free until reviewed.

The fee base uses integer arithmetic and half-up rounding to five USDC decimal places. Values below `0.00001 USDC` are charged as zero.

## Operator taker share

The operator receives a share of the final taker fee. The initial allowed range is `20–45%`, and a new or unregistered builder uses `30%`.

```text
total fee = ceil_5(Kuest fee base × 10,000 / (10,000 - operator share bps))
operator fee = floor(total fee × operator share bps / 10,000)
Kuest fee = total fee - operator fee
```

The gross-up is rounded upward at five decimal places. Integer division dust remains in the Kuest component.

### Example

For `100` Crypto shares at `50¢` and the recommended `30%` operator share:

| Component | Calculation | Fee |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Kuest fee base | `100 × 0.0441 × 0.50 × 0.50` | `$1.10250` |
| Total charged | `ceil_5($1.10250 / 0.70)` | `$1.57500` |
| Operator | `floor($1.57500 × 30%)` | `$0.47250` |
| Kuest | `$1.57500 - $0.47250` | `$1.10250` |

An order without a registered builder pays the same total. Its default operator portion is classified as `UNASSIGNED_BUILDER`, but both components are credited to Kuest. Registering the builder at `30%` changes the receiver split without changing the trader's total fee.

## Maker fee

The maker setting is independent and flat on filled cash value. It starts at `0%` and is initially capped at `1%` for the operator. Kuest also has an independent maker setting that starts at `0%`.

```text
maker total = Kuest maker flat + operator maker flat
```

<Warning>
Recommended: `0%`. Maker fees may reduce liquidity.
</Warning>

## Affiliate split

Affiliate percentage applies only inside the operator amount:

```text
affiliate payout = operator fee × affiliate share
operator retained fee = operator fee - affiliate payout
```

It does not change what the trader pays and never reduces the Kuest component.

## Maker Rebates and Liquidity Rewards

- Maker Rebates reserve the category percentage from confirmed Kuest fee amounts for each market and daily epoch.
- Liquidity Rewards use eligible Kuest fee budget for configured markets.
- Operator, affiliate, maker-flat, and `UNASSIGNED_BUILDER` amounts do not fund either program.

Maker Rebates and Liquidity Rewards are independent. A resting order can earn a Liquidity Reward and also receive a Maker Rebate when it fills.

## Who funds what

| Kuest funds | The operator funds |
| --- | --- |
| Maker Rebates, Liquidity Rewards, protocol-owned liquidity (POL), and backend infrastructure | Affiliate payouts, when enabled, frontend infrastructure, and marketing |

## Operational changes

Range, default share, maker caps, Kuest maker fee, and `maxFeeRateBps` are Exchange-admin settings. Change both Exchanges together while trading is paused, verify their getters, let the CLOB and orderbook subgraph synchronize, and resume only after both report the same policy.
