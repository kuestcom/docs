Kuest applies a dynamic protocol fee when a taker order is matched. Your site can add an operator fee to maker and taker fills, and can share part of that operator fee with affiliates.

<Info>
Kuest maker fee is `0%`. Kuest's taker fee varies by market category and execution price. The fee parameters are applied at match time and are not included in the signed order.
</Info>

## Kuest taker fee

The Kuest component is calculated for each fill:

```text
Kuest fee = shares × rate × [price × (1 - price)]^exponent
```

Where:

- `shares` is the number of shares filled.
- `price` is the execution price expressed from `0` to `1`.
- `rate` is determined by the market category.
- `exponent` is currently `1`.

The rate is a formula parameter, not a flat percentage of trade value. Fees are highest around `50¢` and decrease toward `0¢` and `100¢`. With exponent `1`, the Kuest fee is symmetric: a fill at `30¢` has the same Kuest fee in USDC as a fill at `70¢` for the same number of shares.

Mirrored markets follow their active Polymarket fee schedule. Kuest-native markets use their assigned category, while an unclassified native market remains fee-free until it is reviewed.

### Precision

Kuest fees are rounded to five decimal places. A calculated fee below `0.00001 USDC` is charged as zero.

## Operator fees

Your site can configure independent maker and taker fees from `0%` to `1%`. These are flat percentages of filled notional:

```text
filled notional = shares × execution price
operator fee = filled notional × operator fee rate
```

The trader's total fee is the sum of the applicable components:

```text
total taker fee = Kuest dynamic taker fee + operator taker fee
total maker fee = operator maker fee
```

We recommend starting with:

| Operator setting | Recommended value |
| --- | ---: |
| Taker fee | `0.5%` |
| Maker fee | `0%` |

Keeping the maker fee at zero avoids discouraging resting liquidity. The operator taker fee remains additional to the Kuest curve; it does not reduce or replace the Kuest component.

### Example

Consider a Crypto taker fill of `100` shares at `50¢`, with an operator taker fee of `0.5%`:

| Component | Calculation | Fee |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Kuest | `100 × 0.07 × 0.50 × 0.50` | `$1.75` |
| Operator | `$50 filled notional × 0.5%` | `$0.25` |
| **Total charged** | `$1.75 + $0.25` | **`$2.00`** |

## Maker Rebates and Liquidity Rewards

The Kuest fee and operator fee have separate destinations:

- **Maker Rebates** reserve the category's rebate percentage from confirmed Kuest taker fees in each market and daily epoch. Eligible maker fills share that pool proportionally.
- **Liquidity Rewards** use the remaining eligible Kuest fee budget after the Maker Rebate reserve to reward qualifying resting orders near the midpoint.
- **Operator and affiliate fees never fund either program.** They belong to the operator fee flow.

Maker Rebates and Liquidity Rewards are independent. An order can earn Liquidity Rewards while it rests and also earn a Maker Rebate if it fills as maker.

<Warning>
Do not calculate either rewards pool from the trader's combined fee. Only the Kuest component funds Kuest Maker Rebates and Liquidity Rewards.
</Warning>

  <Accordion title="Affiliate share (%)" icon="hand-coins">

  <Accordion title="Claimable fees" icon="coins">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Totals and affiliate performance" icon="table-properties">

  </Accordion>

Affiliate payouts are calculated only from the operator component:

```text
affiliate payout = operator fee × affiliate share
operator retained fee = operator fee - affiliate payout
```
